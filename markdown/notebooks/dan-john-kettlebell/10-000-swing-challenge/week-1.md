## 10,000 Swing Challenge — Week 1

### Description

Week 1 of the 10,000 Swing Challenge: five workouts, 500 swings each (2,500 total). Each workout is five rounds of 10, 15, 25, and 50 two-hand swings, with one goblet squat between rounds.

```time
(5)
  10 KB Swing 24kg
  15 KB Swing 24kg
  25 KB Swing 24kg
  50 KB Swing 24kg
  :30 Rest
  1 Goblet Squat 24kg
```

### Breakdown

- **Rounds/Duration**: 5 workouts × 5 rounds
- **Exercises**: Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing, Goblet Squat
- **Goal**: 2,500 swings this week; 10,000 total

### Collectible Metrics

- Total time per workout
- Weight used

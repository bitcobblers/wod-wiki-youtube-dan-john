## 10,000 Swing Challenge — Week 4

### Description

Week 4 of the 10,000 Swing Challenge: five workouts, 500 swings each (2,500 total, 10,000 cumulative). Each workout is five rounds of 10, 15, 25, and 50 two-hand swings, with four goblet squats between rounds.

```time
(5)
  10 KB Swing 24kg
  15 KB Swing 24kg
  25 KB Swing 24kg
  50 KB Swing 24kg
  :30 Rest
  4 Goblet Squat 24kg
```

### Breakdown

- **Rounds/Duration**: 5 workouts × 5 rounds
- **Exercises**: Two-Hand Kettlebell Swing, Goblet Squat
- **Goal**: 2,500 swings this week; 10,000 total — challenge complete

### Collectible Metrics

- Total time per workout
- Weight used
- Strength movement progress
